## Module summaries

**mod-001 — Customer Discovery → PMF.** Falsifiable-assumption framing
(Blank), interview craft (Fitzpatrick's *Mom Test*), JTBD synthesis
(Christensen) and opportunity trees (Torres), PMF measurement with more than
one instrument (Sean-Ellis + cohorts + pull), and the classic discovery
anti-patterns. Currently authored.

**mod-002 — Opportunity Assessment and Ruthless Prioritization.** Cagan-shape
opportunity assessments, RICE / WSJF / Kano choice, multi-surface ranking (the
Archive / commercetools problem), and the kill-list discipline most PMs skip.
Bounds the prioritization by runway (defers to
[finance & fundraising](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum)
for the runway math).

**mod-003 — Roadmap Craft: Outcomes over Output, Multi-Quarter Strategy.**
Rumelt's diagnosis-guiding-policy-coherent-action test, Perri-shape outcomes
roadmaps, OKRs at CPO scope, platform-vs-point-product bets, and three
separately-authored roadmap read-outs (board, GTM, team). Handles roadmap
disagreement with a technical founder-CEO.

**mod-004 — Discovery / Delivery Cadence.** Dual-track Cagan/Torres rhythm,
Shape Up vs. weekly kanban at pre-seed/seed scale, PRDs in Amazon-6-pager
shape, IC design taste (Figma + microcopy), and agentic-UX iteration loops for
AI-native products. Diagnoses ship-velocity anti-patterns.

**mod-005 — Metrics, Experimentation, and Eval-Driven Product Decisions.**
Product-analytics regime beyond PMF (activation / retention / cohort curves,
funnel drop-off), A/B experiment design with MDE sizing and guard metrics, and
eval-driven decisions for LLM products (Hamel Husain evals, LLM-as-judge with
calibration, cost/latency/quality Pareto). **Folds AI/LLM-native product
management** (the market's fastest-growing 2026 competency — 66% of postings).

**mod-006 — Pricing, Packaging, and Monetization.** Ramanujam-shape
willingness-to-pay research, good/better/best packaging, pricing experiments
with guard metrics, and AI-product-specific monetization (cost-plus vs.
value-based, credit / metering, DPA-gated enterprise motions).

**mod-007 — Working with Founders, Engineering, and GTM.** The direct-with-
technical-CEO working relationship the market asks for in ~56% of postings —
decision-rights maps, technical fluency (links to
[cto-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum) for
architectural depth), AI-system-architecture read-through (links to
[ai-eval-engineer](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning),
[rag-engineer](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/rag-engineer-learning),
[agentic-ai-engineer](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-ai-engineer-learning)),
and cross-functional influence without authority.

## Ownership rule

Assign primary coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the
skill. Higher-level tracks link to the owner unless additional depth,
architectural context, or leadership scope is required. See
[JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md#ownership-rule-reminder) for the
level ladder and
[.aicg/curriculum-plan.json](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json) `ownership_rule` for
the full narrative.

Deferrals this repo makes:

- **Down** — ambiguity / trait-level competencies → [startup-foundations](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations)
  (level 10); founder-background signal → [founder-ceo-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum)
  (level 20).
- **Sideways** — engineering-leadership craft → [cto-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum)
  (level 25); GTM / enterprise-selling motion / growth-PLG loops →
  [startup-product-gtm-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum)
  (level 30); board-facing narrative / fundraising →
  [startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum)
  (level 40); first-hiring / people-ops depth →
  [startup-operations-governance-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum)
  (level 50).
- **Up** — CPO-of-portfolio / product-of-AI-products scope →
  [chief-ai-officer-learning](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning)
  (level 70); M&A / secondary-transaction depth →
  [startup-exit-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-exit-curriculum)
  (level 60).
- **Out of scope** — legal counsel, external auditors, patent / immigration /
  tax specialists; and vertical domain expertise (fintech, healthtech, biotech,
  security, resale, logistics), which is candidate-supplied.
