# Founding CPO / Head of Product Curriculum — Role Pathway

> **How do I discover, define, prioritize, and ship product?**

A role *pathway* over the shared functional curricula. Start with
[startup-foundations](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations),
then work the pillars below at your stage.

## Target coverage

| Pillar | Target | Owning curriculum |
|---|----|----|
| Foundations | 100% | [startup-foundations](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) |
| Product | 100% | this repo |
| GTM / Sales / Marketing | 80% | [product & GTM](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum) |
| Strategy | 60% | [founder-ceo](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum) |
| People | 50% | [operations & governance](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) |
| Technical Leadership | 40% | [cto-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum) |
| Finance | 30% | [finance & fundraising](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum) |
| Exit & Endgame | 30% | [exit & endgame](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-exit-curriculum) |

## Product modules (this repo owns)

Customer discovery → PMF · opportunity assessment & prioritization · roadmap and
outcomes vs. output · discovery/delivery cadence · metrics & experimentation ·
pricing & packaging as product · working with founders, eng, and GTM.
