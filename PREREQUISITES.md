# Prerequisites for the Founding CPO / Head of Product track

The Founding CPO / Head of Product track sits at **level 35** in the Startup
Leadership family. It assumes shared foundational work and a working command of
the peer engineering / founder tracks. Everything below is either satisfied by
another curriculum in the ecosystem or by equivalent professional experience.

## Assumed prior tracks

- [**startup-foundations**](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations)
  (level 10) — company formation, stage vocabulary (pre-seed / seed / Series A /
  B), unit-economics basics (LTV, CAC, contribution margin), and the shared
  functional-curricula map that every Startup Leadership track references.
- [**founder-ceo-curriculum**](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum)
  (level 20) — company-thesis authoring, capital-allocation vocabulary, and the
  CEO-side of the CPO/CEO working relationship. The founding CPO consumes the
  thesis; the CEO authors it.

Complete these before starting mod-001, or bring equivalent lived experience
from a prior startup founding-team seat.

## Assumed working knowledge

You should walk in with:

- **Product intuition from at least one prior 0→1** — you have shipped
  *something* to real users, whether as a PM, engineer, designer, founder, or
  operator. If you have not, work through
  [startup-foundations](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations)
  first.
- **Enough SQL to write your own analytics queries** — you can write a
  cohort-retention query against an events table without pairing. mod-005
  builds on this; it does not teach it.
- **Enough technical fluency to read a PR** — you do not need to write
  production code, but you can read a pull request, follow a service diagram,
  and reason about an API contract at a level that lets you discuss trade-offs
  with the founding-engineer team. mod-007 sharpens this; it does not build it
  from zero. If you need to build it,
  [cto-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum)
  (level 25, sideways peer) mod-102 (Architecture Under Uncertainty) and
  mod-103 (Build-vs-Buy) are the fastest path.
- **Working command of at least one design tool** — Figma is the market
  default. mod-004 assumes you can ship a mockup and sweat microcopy yourself
  in the pre-seed / seed cadence; it does not teach Figma from zero.
- **AI/LLM system literacy at the user level** — you have used current
  frontier models (Claude, GPT, Gemini) daily, prompted an agentic tool, and
  formed opinions on where the technology helps and where it fails. Deeper
  system-side architecture is folded into mod-005 and mod-007 (and defers to
  [ai-eval-engineer-learning](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/ai-eval-engineer-learning),
  [rag-engineer-learning](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/rag-engineer-learning),
  [agentic-ai-engineer-learning](https://github.com/ai-engineering-curriculum/agentic-ai-engineer-learning)
  for depth).

## What this curriculum does *not* teach

Deferred to other tracks by the ownership rule
(see [JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md#ownership-rule-reminder)):

- **Base 0→1 GTM mechanics, enterprise-selling motions, growth-PLG loops** →
  [startup-product-gtm-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum)
  (level 30). This repo covers the CPO-side coordination and the pricing /
  packaging surface, not the sales-motion mechanics themselves.
- **Engineering-leadership craft — architecture, first engineering hires,
  technical debt, founder-scope security** →
  [cto-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum)
  (level 25). The CPO consumes the CTO's decisions and coordinates on scope;
  the CTO owns the engineering craft.
- **Board reporting / fundraising narrative construction** →
  [startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum)
  (level 40). The CPO authors the product-progress slice of the board
  narrative; the CEO / CFO own the fundraising story.
- **Building / hiring the first product team** →
  [startup-operations-governance-curriculum](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum)
  (level 50). Below the 0.30 frequency threshold in 2026 postings; primary
  ownership sits with the ops / governance track.
- **Vertical / domain expertise** (fintech, healthtech, biotech, security,
  resale, logistics) — candidate-supplied, not curriculum-taught. Bring your
  own domain sophistication or acquire it through targeted reading and design
  partners.
- **Legal opinion, external audit, patent / immigration / tax specialty** —
  brief counsel and specialists; do not produce the opinion yourself.

## Recommended reading before mod-001

The mod-001 lectures already cite the canonical texts. If you want to
front-load, read these three in this order:

1. Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*, self-published, 2013. Short. Do not skip.
2. Marty Cagan, *Inspired* (2nd ed.), Wiley, 2017 — the shared vocabulary the
   rest of the curriculum assumes.
3. Teresa Torres, *Continuous Discovery Habits*, Product Talk, 2021 — the
   dual-track discovery / delivery rhythm mod-004 builds on.

If you want the strategy vocabulary early, add Richard Rumelt's *Good Strategy
/ Bad Strategy* (Crown, 2011) before mod-003.
